The rules in the app

The five rule sets (EU261, UK261, US DOT, Canada APPR, Montreal Convention) are bundled as data with a version and a review date, shown in Settings → Legal → Rule sets in use. Rules change; the app may not yet reflect a change made after that date. Check the official sources linked there.

Your claim

You decide whether, when and to whom to send a claim. The claim letter is a draft you are responsible for; read it before sending. The app never sends anything itself.
