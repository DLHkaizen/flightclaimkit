Hỏi đáp

Can I try it without a flight?

On the first screen tap Try a sample incident: a fictional airline, kept in memory only.

Will I get compensation?

The app cannot say that. It shows which rights may apply under which rule, with the article and the rules version. The airline, an authority or a court decides.

Why "Doors open"?

Arrival delay is measured to the moment the aircraft doors open, not touchdown (Court of Justice of the EU, C-452/13).

Does it need the internet?

No. It works offline and sends nothing anywhere. The links on an airport card (the airport's website, Wikipedia, Maps) open in your browser or in Maps, only when you tap them.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your iPhone only. Settings → Your data → Export my data saves one file you keep; Reset the app removes everything. Deleting the app does the same. There is no copy anywhere else.

Which rules does it know?

EU261, UK261, US DOT, Canada APPR and the Montreal Convention, bundled in the app with their versions and official sources (Settings → Legal → Rule sets in use).

What is the Flight Log?

Scan your boarding pass on any flight and tap Save to flight log. The app adds up kilometres, laps of the Earth, countries and airports. If something goes wrong later, the flight is already there: report an incident on it in one tap.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is general information. The app is not affiliated with any airline or authority and files nothing on your behalf.
