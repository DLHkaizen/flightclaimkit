Questions

Can I try it without a flight? Yes: on the first screen tap Try a sample incident. It uses a fictional airline and is kept in memory only.

Does it need the internet? No. It works offline, even in flight mode, and sends nothing anywhere.

Why "Doors open"? Under EU and UK rules, arrival delay is measured to the moment the aircraft doors open, not touchdown. Tap it the moment they open.

Will I get compensation? The app cannot say that. It shows which rights may apply under which rule, with the article and the rules version. The airline, an authority or a court decides.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Your data → Reset the app. There is no copy anywhere else.
