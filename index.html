Flight Claim Kit

Know your rights by the minute

Flight delayed? Watch your rights unlock minute by minute, seal your evidence, and build a claim you file yourself, with no commission. No account. Works offline.

Ứng dụng cho iPhone · sắp có trên App Store

The Rights Clock

A ring shows how late you are and every right on the way — meals, a refund, compensation — with the rule set, the article and the minutes left.

Sealed evidence

Photos, gate announcements, the airline's messages and receipts, each time-stamped and linked in a SHA-256 hash chain. Change one byte and the seal shows it.

A claim pack you file yourself

One PDF with your timeline, evidence, expenses and deadlines, and a claim letter in English. No commission.

Five rule sets, honest answers

EU261, UK261, US DOT, Canada APPR and the Montreal Convention: likely, unlikely, needs one answer, or does not apply. Information, not legal advice.

No account, no network

Everything stays on your iPhone until you share it.
