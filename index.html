Flight Claim Kit

Your flight's black box. Know your rights by the minute.

When a flight is delayed, cancelled, overbooked or your bag does not arrive, Flight Claim Kit shows which passenger rights may have opened, minute by minute; seals your evidence in a SHA-256 hash chain; and builds a claim pack — a PDF and an English letter — that you file yourself, with no commission.

What it does

  • Scan the barcode on your boarding pass (paper, phone or screenshot), or type the flight.
  • The Rights Clock shows how late you are and each right on the way, with the rule set, the article and the minutes left. A Live Activity keeps it in the Dynamic Island.
  • Sealed evidence: photos, gate announcements, the airline's messages, receipts, and one-tap moments such as "Doors open" — each time-stamped and hash-chained.
  • Five rule sets, bundled as versioned data: EU261, UK261, US DOT, Canada APPR, Montreal Convention. Honest outcomes: likely, unlikely, needs one answer, does not apply.
  • No account, no network. Everything stays on your iPhone until you share it.
