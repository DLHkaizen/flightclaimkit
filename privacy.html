Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Flight Claim Kit collects no data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no networking code.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The fields read from your boarding pass (name, booking reference, flight, seat). The barcode's raw text is not kept.
  • Photos, recordings, screenshots and receipts you record as evidence, with their time stamps and hashes.
  • Your claimant details (name, email, postal address), if you enter them, used only to fill in your claim letter.

When anything leaves your iPhone

Only when you tap Share yourself — a claim pack or an export — and only to the place you choose in the iOS share sheet. The app sends nothing on its own.

Permissions

The camera is used to scan your boarding pass and photograph evidence; the microphone to record gate announcements. Both are optional: without them you can still import screenshots and type your flight. Notifications are local, scheduled on your iPhone. The app does not use your location and does not ask for your photo library (screenshots are picked one at a time).

Deleting

Deleting the app, or Settings → Your data → Reset the app, removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else, so it cannot be recovered.
